Our Story

We believe beauty
begins with care

Serenity Studio AU was founded with one vision — to create a haven where women can escape, unwind, and leave feeling their most beautiful. Nestled in Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast, we specialise in lash and brow treatments designed to enhance what you already have.

Every appointment is a personal experience. We take the time to understand your lifestyle, eye shape, and natural lash health before crafting the perfect look for you.

  • Certified & insured lash technician
  • Premium products — Elleebana, YUMI & Borboleta
  • Patch tests available for sensitive clients
  • Private, calm studio environment

Classic Extensions
Lash Extensions

A single extension applied to each natural lash for a subtle, mascara-like finish. Perfect for those new to extensions or seeking an everyday look.

From A$120 / full set
Hybrid Extensions
Lash Extensions

A mix of classic and volume lashes creating a textured, wispy effect. Ideal for clients wanting more volume and drama than classic but softer than a full volume set.

From A$140 / full set
Volume Extensions
Lash Extensions

Multiple ultra-fine lashes fanned and applied to a single natural lash for maximum drama and fullness. The ultimate glam look.

From A$160 / full set
Lash Lift & Tint
Natural Lash Treatment

Curl and define your natural lashes with a semi-permanent lift, paired with a tint to darken and define. No extensions needed. Results last 6–8 weeks.

From A$95 / treatment
Brow Lamination
Brow Treatment

Sleek, brushed-up brows that stay in place all day. Brow lamination restructures the brow hairs for a fuller, more defined shape. Includes tint & shape.

From A$90 / treatment
Brow Shape & Tint
Brow Treatment

A precise wax and thread shaping tailored to your natural brow structure, combined with a customised tint to add depth and definition that lasts 3–4 weeks.

From A$55 / treatment
